# First Night

Build **First Night**, a polished browser-native 3D voxel survival game with Three.js, inspired by the exploration, block breaking and placement, crafting and night survival of **Minecraft** without copying its protected assets. Generate an original compact island where the player gathers wood and stone, crafts a signal beacon, builds a genuinely enclosed shelter, places the beacon on its roof and survives hostile creatures until dawn, with satisfying world interaction, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and a clear win, loss and restart flow. Minimum: `npm run build` must succeed and emit a playable `dist/`.
