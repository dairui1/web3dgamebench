# Turbo Circuit

Build **Turbo Circuit**, a polished browser-native 3D arcade kart racer with Three.js, inspired by the colorful circuits, drift boosts, items and rival racing of **Mario Kart** without copying its protected assets. Create an original looping track where the player races several AI opponents over multiple laps, earns speed through well-timed drifts, uses offensive and defensive items, discovers alternate lines and fights for first place, with lively feedback, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and a clear finish and restart flow. Minimum: `npm run build` must succeed and emit a playable `dist/`.
