# Canyon Strike

Build **Canyon Strike**, a polished browser-native 3D arcade air-combat mission with Three.js, inspired by the third-person flying, targeting and missile combat of **Ace Combat** without copying its protected assets. Let the player pilot an original aircraft through a dramatic mountain canyon, fight both ground and airborne threats, then reach extraction after completing the strike, with satisfying flight handling, clear target and damage feedback, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and an unambiguous win, loss and restart flow.
