# Star Course

Build **Star Course**, a polished browser-native 3D platform game with Three.js, inspired by the free-running movement, follow camera, collectibles, enemies and goal-star structure of **Super Mario 64** without copying its protected assets. Create an original compact course that carries the player from a grassy opening across a moving-platform ravine to a summit, rewarding coin collection, skillful enemy interaction and confident traversal before the final star, with expressive movement, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and a clear win, loss and restart flow.
