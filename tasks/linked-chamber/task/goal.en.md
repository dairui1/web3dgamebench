# Linked Chamber

Build **Linked Chamber**, a polished browser-native 3D first-person spatial puzzle with Three.js, inspired by the paired-portal traversal and momentum puzzles of **Portal 2** without copying its protected assets. Create one original test chamber where the player places a linked portal pair on suitable surfaces, redirects their movement, carries a cube through the portals, uses it to hold a floor switch and reaches the locked exit, with readable spatial cues, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and a clear completion and restart flow. A complete game must open and be playable; an unfinished scaffold is not a submission.
