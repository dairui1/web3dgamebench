# Dinner Rush

Build **Dinner Rush**, a polished browser-native 3D kitchen coordination game with Three.js, inspired by the hectic station-to-station teamwork of **Overcooked** without copying its protected assets. Let one player switch between two chefs in an original compact restaurant kitchen, prepare several kinds of timed orders through distinct cooking steps, recover from mistakes and deliver enough correct dishes before closing, with readable stations and order feedback, responsive desktop and mobile controls, and a clear win, loss and restart flow. Minimum: `npm run build` must succeed and emit a playable `dist/`.
