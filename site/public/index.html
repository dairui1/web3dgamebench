Frozen task

Every profile receives identical candidate-visible files. The complete matrix closes before any source is published.

02

Isolated build

Disposable workspaces have an offline dependency store and model-API-only egress. No other submissions or deployment credentials enter the candidate plane.

03

Playable checks

Build, canvas output, input, resize, restart, mobile controls, and a small runtime-state contract establish basic reliability.

04

Human ranking

Automation cannot decide whether a game feels good. Blind pairwise play supplies the public ranking.
